# Review playbook

1. Establish change scope and the relevant baseline.
2. Inspect the full diff and impacted callers, jobs, assets, or dashboards.
3. Prioritize correctness, security, data loss, permission boundaries, and regressions.
4. Reproduce concrete concerns when safe; don't present speculation as a confirmed bug.
5. Distinguish blocking findings from nonblocking recommendations.
6. Report findings by severity with file/location, evidence, fix guidance, and verification gaps.
